The problem

Agents don't fail the way apps fail.

Most security stacks were built to watch people click through a browser. AI agents call APIs directly, at machine speed, with credentials that outlive any one task — and that's exactly where the blind spots are.

01

Shadow AI inside trusted SaaS tenants

Employees and teams connect AI copilots, plug-ins and automation bots straight into SaaS apps — outside any provisioning process, invisible to existing IAM and CASB tools.

02

Bearer tokens don't prove who's calling

A static API key or long-lived OAuth token can be replayed from anywhere. Without proof-of-possession, "authenticated" doesn't mean "the agent you think it is."

03

Compliance as a snapshot, not a state

A point-in-time audit says nothing about the agent that got a new grant, or started behaving differently, the day after the report shipped.

The platform

Four planes, one control point.

Observable's agentless architecture layers identity, discovery, behavior and compliance over the SaaS and AI agents you already run — with a single place to see and act on all of it.

Agent Guard

Strong identity for every agent

Certificate-bound identities and proof-of-possession tokens replace bearer credentials, so a stolen token alone can't impersonate an agent.

Inventory & Posture

Find what you didn't provision

Agentless SaaS scanning surfaces shadow-AI integrations and posture gaps — stale admin accounts, missing MFA, over-broad agent grants — and tracks drift as it happens.

Detection

Risk scored from real behavior

A learned behavioral baseline per agent turns anomalies — not manually written rules — into a live risk score, catching a hijacked session mid-burst.

Compliance & Export

Prove it, continuously

A control-by-control compliance report regenerates from live state, with CEF/JSON audit export and SOAR-ready incident records for your existing SIEM/SOAR.

Framework alignment

Mapped to the NIST AI RMF, not just labeled with it.

Observable's four planes sit on the NIST AI Risk Management Framework's own structure: GOVERN wraps the cycle as a cross-cutting function, while MAP, MEASURE and MANAGE run as a continuous loop underneath it.

  • MAP — Inventory & Posture discovers what exists and characterizes impact.
  • MEASURE — Detection quantifies risk from behavior, continuously.
  • MANAGE — Agent Guard acts: policy decisions, containment, revocation.
  • GOVERN — Compliance & Export reads and reports on all three, live.
